Partners

Built alongside the people who use it.

Kinetra's Quick Tests and movement checks are shaped by direct input from clinicians and researchers, and Kinetra Community exists to work with real places -- senior centers, clinics, schools, and teams. This page is where those organizations will be listed as those relationships are formalized.

Become a partner See Kinetra Community
What a partnership looks like

A few different shapes, same goal.

Kinetra partners with organizations in a few different ways, depending on what makes sense for them.

Clinical & Research

Grounded in real protocols

Clinicians and researchers whose published methods and direct feedback shape what Kinetra measures and how -- the Quick Tests screens are one example of this in practice.

Community Sites

Free check-ins, on-site

Senior centers, clinics, and schools hosting free Kinetra Community mobility check-in sessions for the people they serve.

Teams & Programs

Tracking a group over time

Coaches and programs using Team Screening to check a roster's movement over a season, not just one person's.

Just getting started

No partners listed publicly yet.

This page is new. As relationships with clinics, research labs, schools, and community organizations are formalized, they'll be named here -- nothing listed before it's real.
