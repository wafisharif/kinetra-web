Why Kinetra

Where generic fitness apps stop.

Rep-counters tell you what you did. Kinetra tells you how well you did it, and whether that's changing over time.

01

Camera-only pose tracking

MediaPipe-based pose estimation runs on a normal video recording. No wearables, no special hardware needed.

02

Per-limb calibration

A one-time calibration flow learns your own observed range of motion per arm, so rep-detection thresholds come from that instead of a generic default.

03

Honest, non-diagnostic coaching

An optional AI coaching layer reads your scores and trends, never your video, and says plainly that it isn't a medical diagnosis.

Task Coverage

One camera, six movement tests.

Every mode uses the same pose pipeline, tuned to the joints and rep pattern that matter for that task.

SIT-TO-STAND

Chair-rise strength & control

Tracks hip and knee extension across each rise, flagging asymmetric weight-bearing and rushed, uncontrolled descents.

  • Rep count & tempo per rise
  • Left/right knee-extension symmetry
  • Descent-control smoothness score
Product

From a single recording to a movement history.

Mobile

Kinetra App

The Expo / React Native client: guided recording, calibration, a Daily Check-In log, Trend and Insight screens, and a Team Screening mode for coaches running batches of athletes.

Backend

Analysis Engine

A Flask + MediaPipe service that turns a video into joint angles, rep counts, and per-task scores. It's deployed on Render, with an optional server-side AI coaching endpoint that never sees raw video.

How it Works

Calibrated to your own range of motion.

CALIBRATION

Kinetra Calibration

Most rep-counters assume everyone's "full range of motion" looks the same. Kinetra records your own observed angle range per arm during a short calibration pass, then derives rep-detection thresholds from that, so a smaller natural range isn't scored as a failed rep.

See it in the beta
  • 01
    Record a short baseline
    A few reps of the task at a comfortable range.
  • 02
    Calibrate per arm
    Left and right thresholds are stored independently.
  • 03
    Analyze against your baseline
    Every future session is scored relative to your own range.
  • 04
    Recalibrate any time
    Range of motion changes during recovery, and your baseline can change with it.

Per-side persistence

Left and right calibration values are stored and applied independently, so one side's data can never silently override the other's.

Six task profiles

Sit-to-stand, reach, arm raise, gait, balance, and timed-up-and-go each get their own joint focus and thresholds.

Recalibrate anytime

A visible reset in Settings, since recovery is a moving target and needs a baseline that can move with it.

Daily Insights

Fusing how you feel with what the camera measures.

A 30-second, no-camera check-in feeds a rule-based engine that waits for enough evidence before it says anything.

Daily Check-In

A local, no-network log: an overall feeling rating, multi-select soreness by body region, and an optional note. Takes under 30 seconds, and works even on days with no recording.

Insight Engine

Cross-references check-ins against recorded scores: a streak counter, a soreness-to-score correlation per task, and a left/right asymmetry detector, each gated behind a minimum sample size, so it only speaks when there's evidence.

Evidence over engagement

A correlation only surfaces once there are at least two comparable sore and clear sessions within a 36-hour window, with enough of a gap between them to mean something. Without that, nothing shows up.

Who it's for

Built for anyone who wants a number instead of a guess.

01

Coaches & trainers

Screen a whole roster for movement quality with Team Screening, and see who needs a second look without chasing everyone down individually.

02

Home rehab

Doing guided exercises at home and want an objective number instead of guessing whether today was actually better than last week.

03

Curious self-trackers

Wondering whether your range of motion, balance, or day-to-day feeling is really changing week to week, beyond how any single day feels.

04

Student-athletes & teams

Track consistency across a season, build a check-in streak, and give a coach a shared view without a spreadsheet.
