Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026
In one paragraph: Kinetra collects the minimum data needed to compute and show you a movement score, keeps almost everything on your own device, and runs no ads and no third-party analytics or tracking of any kind. The app itself has no account system. The one thing that leaves your device by default is a video clip sent to Kinetra's own analysis server for processing, deleted immediately after. The one optional thing that can leave your device is a small set of numbers sent to a third-party AI service, and only if you tap "Ask AI" yourself. A separate, optional web dashboard does have its own sign-in accounts. See Section 4.

1. Video and camera data

When you record a movement check, the video is uploaded to Kinetra's analysis backend over HTTPS for the sole purpose of running pose estimation and computing your result.

  • The video file is deleted from the server's disk immediately after processing completes, whether the analysis succeeds or fails.
  • The video is never viewed by a human, never used to train any model, and never shared with, sold to, or disclosed to any third party.
  • No video is stored anywhere long-term, on the server or otherwise.

2. What's stored on your device

The following is saved only in the app's local storage on your own phone, never transmitted anywhere as a matter of course:

  • Session results: numeric scores, letter grades, movement type, timestamp, which side (left/right) was recorded, and (if you're using Team Screening) the name typed in for that check.
  • Your personalized calibration ranges, if set, separately for each side.
  • Your reminder time preference and Consistency Streak / badge progress.
  • A record of whether you've seen onboarding and the current "What's New" screen.

None of this is backed up automatically. The app itself still has no account system, so if you delete the app or clear its data, this information is gone and Kinetra cannot recover it for you.

3. Daily Check-Ins & Insights

Kinetra's optional Daily Check-In lets you log a general feeling rating (1–5) and any sore areas, without recording video. This is stored only in the app's local storage on your device, exactly like the session results above. It is never uploaded, never transmitted to Kinetra's backend, and never sent to any third party.

The Insights screen compares your Daily Check-Ins against your recorded session scores (streaks, soreness-vs-score correlation, left/right asymmetry) entirely on your device. Nothing about this computation leaves your phone.

4. Web dashboard accounts

The browser dashboard at kinetraapp.com/dashboard is separate from the mobile app and is the one part of Kinetra with an account system. Creating an account there means giving a name, an email address, and a password, all sent to Kinetra's backend server over HTTPS. Your password is hashed before it's ever stored, so Kinetra itself cannot see or recover your plain password.

The movement stats, streaks, and sessions currently shown in the dashboard are sample data, since the app does not yet send your actual sessions there. That's on the Roadmap. Your dashboard account and the app's on-device data are not connected today.

You can have your dashboard account and its data deleted at any time by emailing the contact below.

5. AI Coach (optional, off unless configured)

If enabled, tapping "Ask AI for a Personal Note" sends the following to a third-party AI service, and nothing else: the task name, your most recent numeric score, letter grade, and confidence grade; which side you recorded; whether you've calibrated; and up to your last several numeric scores for that same task, for trend context.

It never sends: video, raw pose/joint position data, or any personal identifier beyond a task label you may have typed in yourself. This request goes through Kinetra's own backend server, which holds the API credential, so your phone never does. This feature is opt-in per use and fails closed: if the feature has not been configured, tapping the button tells you so and sends nothing anywhere.

6. Known accuracy limitations

Because this affects what "your data" actually represents: a single 2D camera cannot fully resolve depth, so movement directly toward or away from the camera is measured less reliably than side-to-side movement. Poor lighting, loose clothing, or a joint leaving the frame all reduce tracking accuracy. Kinetra has not been independently validated against a clinical, marker-based motion-capture system.

7. Data about minors

Kinetra's Team Screening mode lets an adult (a coach or teacher) attach a name to someone else's recorded movement check. The app does not distinguish an adult teammate from a minor, and does not collect a parent's or guardian's consent itself. If you're screening anyone under 18, get their parent's or guardian's permission before recording them.

8. No ads, no third-party trackers

Kinetra contains no advertising SDK, no third-party analytics enabled by default, and does not sell or share data with data brokers. An optional, off-by-default crash-reporting integration exists in the app for diagnosing bugs; it ships disabled and reports only error details, never movement data, video, or personal information.

9. Your choices

You can clear all locally stored data by deleting the app, or using the in-app calibration reset and "Clear Check-In History" controls. The app itself has no "export my data" or "delete my account" flow beyond that, since it has no accounts. For the web dashboard account described in Section 4, email the contact below to have it deleted.
