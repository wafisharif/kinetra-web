Kinetra is for movement awareness only. It does not diagnose, treat, predict injury, estimate fall risk, or replace medical, physical therapy, or coaching advice from a qualified professional. Nothing Kinetra produces should be the sole basis for a medical, return-to-play, or safety decision.

1. What Kinetra is

Kinetra is a mobile application that uses your device's camera and a pose-estimation model (Google's MediaPipe) to estimate joint positions during a short recorded movement, and computes scores describing things like range of motion, consistency, and stability from those estimates. It also offers an optional, camera-free Daily Check-In and an on-device Insights feature that looks for patterns between the two.

2. Who can use it

You must be at least 13 years old to use Kinetra yourself. If you're under 18, a parent, guardian, coach, or teacher should be aware you're using it.

Team Screening mode lets one person (a coach or teacher) attach a name to someone else's recorded movement check. The app does not verify anyone's age or collect consent on a screened person's behalf. If you're using Team Screening on anyone under 18, get their parent's or guardian's permission first.

3. Your content

Video you record is uploaded to Kinetra's analysis server solely to compute a result for that one request, and is deleted from the server immediately after processing (see the Privacy Policy for specifics). You retain all rights to any video you record. Kinetra does not claim ownership of it, and does not use it for any purpose beyond generating your result.

4. Web dashboard accounts

The web dashboard at kinetraapp.com/dashboard is a separate, optional part of Kinetra with its own sign-up and sign-in. The same age requirement in Section 2 applies here: you must be at least 13 to create a dashboard account. You're responsible for the accuracy of the name and email you provide and for keeping your password to yourself. If you turn on Web Dashboard Sync from the app's Settings and sign in there with the same dashboard account, the stats and history shown here are your real synced data, as described in the Privacy Policy. Until then, the dashboard has nothing to show.
