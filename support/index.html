Common questions

The app is asking for camera access — why, and is anything recorded without me knowing?

Kinetra needs camera access to record the short movement clips it analyzes. Nothing is recorded until you explicitly start a recording in the app. Recordings are captured muted; audio is never recorded or analyzed.

What happens to my video after I record it?

It's uploaded to Kinetra's analysis server only to compute your result, then deleted immediately — it's never stored long-term, viewed by a person, or used for anything else. Full detail is in the Privacy Policy.

My score seems off, or tracking looks wrong. What's going on?

Camera-based pose estimation has real limits: a single 2D camera can't fully resolve depth, and poor lighting, loose clothing, or a joint leaving the frame can all reduce accuracy. Try recording again with the camera roughly perpendicular to your movement, in good lighting, with the full range of motion staying in frame. Kinetra is a movement-awareness tool, not a clinical measurement device — if you have real concerns about your movement or pain, talk to a doctor, physical therapist, or qualified coach.

Can I get my data, or delete everything Kinetra has stored?

Kinetra doesn't have user accounts, and almost everything (your session history, calibration, Daily Check-Ins) lives only in local storage on your own phone. Deleting the app removes all of it. If you want something handled differently, email us and it'll be taken care of directly.

What's the "AI Coach" feature, and is it required?

It's an optional, opt-in button that sends your recent scores (never video) to an AI service for a short personalized note. It's entirely optional to use, and the app works fully without it.

Is Kinetra a medical device?

No. Kinetra is for movement awareness only, and does not diagnose, treat, or predict any medical condition. See the Terms of Service for the full statement.
