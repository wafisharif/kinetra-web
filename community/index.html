Kinetra Community

The volunteer side of Kinetra.

Kinetra itself is a one-person build. Kinetra Community is the effort to grow past that: free movement check-ins for real people, open volunteer involvement, and funding that's fully visible to anyone who wants to look.

Sponsor us Get involved
Why it exists

Built by one person. Meant for more than one.

Everything else on this site is Kinetra the product. Kinetra Community is the nonprofit-style effort around it -- bringing free movement check-ins to people who'd benefit from them, starting with places like senior and community centers, and opening the door for anyone who wants to help build that.

Transparent Finances

Every dollar in and out is public, through Hack Club Bank's fiscal sponsorship program -- not a promise, a live ledger anyone can check.
